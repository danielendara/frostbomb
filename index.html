Recent Seeds

Temperature

Determines if ice grows into flat plates or long columns. Subtle changes at the molecular level flip the growth between "basal" and "prism" faces.

Supersaturation

The "fuel" for growth. Low humidity creates simple, solid shapes. High humidity forces unstable, rapid growth that branches into complex dendrites.

Habit

The resulting morphological category. From simple "plates" to "fern-like dendrites," the habit is a record of the path taken through the atmosphere.

Branches

The total count of hexagonal ice segments. Higher humidity and longer growth times lead to more intricate, branching structures with thousands of individual segments.

Nakaya Diagram

The physical map of snowflake shapes. Created by Ukichiro Nakaya in the 1930s, it proves that no two snowflakes are alike because no two follow the same path.
